# Мобильный фотофон

Файл: `assets/hero-mobile-photo.webp`. Создан встроенным ImageGen, затем оптимизирован в WebP (900 × 1350). Иллюстративный интерьер, не фотография объекта компании.

## Промпт

Ночная версия: `assets/hero-mobile-night.webp`, создана встроенным ImageGen из дневного фото. Светлая тема показывает день, тёмная — ночь.

Промпт ночной версии: Use case: lighting-weather. Edit the supplied photograph into realistic nighttime. Preserve EXACT camera position, portrait crop, architecture, every window frame and mullion, curtains, furniture, plants and city buildings. Change ONLY lighting and time of day. Outside: deep midnight blue sky, no sunset and no sun, subtle cool moonlight, scattered warm glowing windows and streetlights across the same city, realistic subdued trees. Inside: soft warm indirect lamplight from an unseen source, gentle amber reflections on white window frames and oak floor, dark forest green wall. Clearly night, atmospheric but legible architectural photography, not pitch black. No new furniture, no people, no text, no logo. Full bleed photograph.

Дневная версия:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait mobile website hero background for a premium window manufacturer. Generate a realistic architectural interior photograph, portrait 2:3 composition. A beautiful tall white PVC panoramic window occupies the right two thirds, looking out over a quiet leafy city neighborhood in soft early morning light. Muted sage green plaster wall on the left, subtle linen curtain, a glimpse of oak floor and a small leafy plant at bottom right. Elegant lived-in calm atmosphere, natural glass reflections, precise plausible window frames, editorial interior photography, no CGI look. Composition must leave the left half and upper-left relatively dark, uncluttered and low contrast for white website heading overlaid later, while the window and outdoor view remain clearly visible on the right. Palette deep forest green, sage, warm ivory, natural wood. No people, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no UI. Produce a full bleed photograph, not a mockup.
